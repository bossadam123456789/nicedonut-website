Who We Are

A trading house built on taste, trust and timely delivery.

Nice Donut Ltd is a Nairobi-based import and distribution company dealing in assorted foods and beverages from across Asia, Africa and Europe. From our base at Amco Plaza, Eastleigh — the heart of Kenya's wholesale trade — we move quality products from origin markets into shops, supermarkets and distributors countrywide.

We handle the full chain ourselves: sourcing, importation, clearance, warehousing and last-mile distribution — so our customers get the right stock, at the right price, on time.

Where We Source

Three continents. One dependable pipeline.

Our buying relationships span key production markets in Asia, Africa and Europe, giving our customers variety and price advantage.

🌏

Asian Markets

We import beverages, confectionery, rice, noodles, spices and packaged foods from established manufacturers across East, South and Southeast Asia and the Middle East — markets known for quality, scale and competitive factory pricing.

🇪🇺

European Markets

We import confectionery, dairy products, biscuits and premium packaged foods from established European manufacturers — bringing recognised quality brands to the Kenyan market at competitive landed costs.

🌍

African Markets

We source regional staples, grains, coffee and fast-moving food products from trusted suppliers across the continent — supporting intra-African trade while keeping landed costs low for our Kenyan customers.
